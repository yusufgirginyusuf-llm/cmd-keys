Cmd Keys Privacy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Cmd Keys changes what your keyboard types. To do that it sees each key you press, the moment you press it, on your own computer.

What Cmd Keys does not do

  • It doesn't record, store or log your keystrokes.
  • It doesn't send anything you type over the internet.
  • It doesn't collect analytics, crash reports or personal information.

Internet

The only connection is the Microsoft Store version asking Windows for its license (free trial or purchased). Windows and the Microsoft Store handle that; no keystrokes or settings are included.

What it stores

Only its own settings (the layout you chose, your options, the apps it pauses for and the keyboards you marked), in a file on your computer under %APPDATA%\CmdKeys. Uninstalling the app removes the app; you can delete that folder to remove the settings.
